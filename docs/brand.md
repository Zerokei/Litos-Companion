# Litos Companion identity

Litos Companion shares the MARGIN identity with Litos: two vertical strokes and one horizontal marginal note. A detached square distinguishes the plugin without changing the parent symbol.

Production SVGs and PNG previews are in [assets/brand](../assets/brand/). Use light artwork on light backgrounds and dark artwork on dark backgrounds. The mono symbol must retain the detached square. Lettering is outlined and requires no external font.

Keep the full name **Litos Companion** at first mention. Describe the product as an optional plugin for Litos; heading controls require a compatible theme, while Zen mode and Mermaid enhancement can operate independently.

Use marks at 24px or larger where possible. At smaller sizes, include a text label to distinguish the products. Do not stretch the mark or separate its strokes. Brand colors do not override Obsidian accent preferences.

The shared source is `scripts/build-brand.py` in the Litos repository. Update this repository's copies whenever the shared geometry changes. Source Serif 4 lettering is used under the [SIL Open Font License](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-serif/blob/release/LICENSE.md); no font files are bundled here.

Colors: light background `#F7F6F2`, ink `#242827`, accent `#397F7B`; dark background `#202524`, ink `#F7F6F2`, accent `#82BCB5`.
